The Artist Story

Handmade, Never Mass-Produced.

Ink & Dye Tees was born out of a passion for subversive streetwear, anime culture, and tactile textile art.

The Handmade Process

Every single t-shirt is hand-tied, hand-dyed using eco-conscious vibrant fiber-reactive dyes, and hand-painted or custom heat-pressed with archival inks in small experimental batches in our studio. No two shirts will ever look identical.

Copyright & Ethics

We respect intellectual property. All featured anime and streetwear artwork is either original creations by our in-house illustrators, properly licensed, or custom-printed directly from customer-provided images and fan artwork permissions.
