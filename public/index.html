Our Story

Art You Can Wear. Handmade in the Studio.

Ink & Dye Tees was born out of a passion for indie anime culture, underground streetwear, and tactile craftsmanship. We believe fast fashion is soulless—every tee we make is hand-dyed, hand-painted, and individually inspected.

Using eco-friendly water-based textile inks and custom wash formulas, our pieces achieve a vintage, lived-in feel that gets better with every wash.

The Handmade Process

  • ⚡ 1. Garment Prep: Heavyweight 100% ring-spun cotton sourced for premium drape.
  • 🎨 2. Hand-Dyeing: Custom tie-dye and mineral wash baths mixed by hand.
  • 🖌️ 3. Hand-Painting: Original stencils and brushwork applied with archival textile inks.
  • 🔥 4. Heat-Curing: Permanent heat setting for maximum wash durability.
